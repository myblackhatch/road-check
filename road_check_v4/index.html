Privacy

No accounts, no tracking. Your location is only used if you tap "Use my location once": it stays in the app on this phone, is never sent to FDOT or TomTom, and is forgotten when you close the app. The app talks to: FDOT's camera list (ArcGIS), FDOT camera images (divas.cloud), and TomTom if you add a key. Each of those can see your IP address, like any website.
